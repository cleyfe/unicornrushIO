## What the AGPL means here

- You may use, study, modify, and share the code.
- If you distribute a modified version, **or run one as a website people can use**,
  you must share its full source code under the AGPL too. That includes linking to the
  source from the game (UnicornRush links to it from the title screen and top bar).
- Keep the "© UnicornRush and Contributors" notice visible, and don't present a
  modified version as the official UnicornRush (see the Section 7 terms in `LICENSE`).

## Inspiration

UnicornRush's gameplay is inspired by territory games such as
[OpenFront](https://github.com/openfrontio/OpenFrontIO). UnicornRush contains no
OpenFront code or assets.
