Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Short version

UnicornRush doesn't ask for or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no purchases, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies. The game runs entirely in your browser; only the background map is downloaded from a third-party map service (see below).

2. Who we are

The Service at https://unicornrush.io is run by [operator name] ("we", "us").

3. What we collect

  • Game data: the company name you type and everything that happens in a match stay in your browser. They are not sent to us and disappear when you close or reload the page.
  • Server logs: like any website, the company that hosts the game ([hosting provider]) may automatically log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are kept for a short time and are not used to identify or profile you.
  • Map tiles: the background map is loaded from OpenFreeMap, so your browser sends it standard request data (such as your IP address) when it downloads map images. We don't control that service; see its own privacy information.

4. Cookies and local storage

The game does not set cookies. If a future version saves settings in your browser (for example your preferred game speed), that data stays on your device.

5. Sharing

We don't sell or share personal data. We only share server logs if the law requires it.

6. Children

The Service is meant for people aged 13 and over. Because we don't collect personal information, we don't knowingly hold any data about children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data about you. Since the game stores none, there is usually nothing to access; for server logs, contact us below.

8. Changes

If we add features that collect data (such as multiplayer or accounts), we'll update this policy first and change the date above.

9. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/cleyfe/unicornrushIO or email [contact email].
