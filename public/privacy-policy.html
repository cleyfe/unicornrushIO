Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Short version

UnicornRush doesn't ask for any personal information. There are no accounts, no purchases, no ads and no tracking cookies. We count visits and games anonymously (how many people open the game, how many matches start and finish, on which city), without any player identifier. Online matches share the company name you choose with the other players in your room. The background map comes from a third-party map service (see below). The iPhone app works the same way: it collects nothing more than the website and doesn't track you across apps.

2. Who we are

The Service, the website https://unicornrush.io and the UnicornRush iPhone app, is run by [operator name] ("we", "us").

3. What we collect

  • Game data: in a single-player match, the company name you type and everything that happens stay in your browser. In an online match, your company name and your moves go through our game server to the other players in your room, for as long as the match runs. We don't store them after the room closes.
  • Anonymous game statistics: when the game page loads we count a visit (and whether it came from a room invite link); when a match starts and ends we record the city, difficulty, number of players and bots, the day it ended and whether a human or a bot won, plus the country the request came from (as reported by our hosting provider). These records contain no name, no IP address and no identifier for you or your device, so they can't be linked to you.
  • Server logs: like any website, the company that hosts the game (Cloudflare) may automatically log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are kept for a short time and are not used to identify or profile you.
  • Map tiles: the background map is loaded from OpenFreeMap, so your browser sends it standard request data (such as your IP address) when it downloads map images. We don't control that service; see its own privacy information.

4. Cookies and local storage

The game does not set cookies. It saves a few things in your browser's local storage (in the app, its own storage on your phone): your last game settings and company name, your map theme, which tips you've seen, and a random key for each online room you join, so you can reconnect to your company. This data stays on your device and is never used to identify you.

5. Sharing

We don't sell or share personal data. We only share server logs if the law requires it.

6. Children

The Service is meant for people aged 13 and over. Because we don't collect personal information, we don't knowingly hold any data about children.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data about you. Since the game stores none, there is usually nothing to access; for server logs, contact us below.

8. Changes

If we add features that collect data (such as multiplayer or accounts), we'll update this policy first and change the date above.
